# Memeline Community Guidelines

Memeline is for sharing memes, talking about where they came from, and
what was happening in the world when they took off. Some of those
events are tragedies that real people lived through. Joke about memes, not
about the people who were hurt.

These guidelines apply to every post, comment and vote. Breaking them can
get your posts removed and your account suspended or banned. See the
[Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md).

## Not allowed

- **Harassment and bullying.** No attacking, threatening or targeting
  anyone, including other users and the people who appear in memes.
- **Hate.** No attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, nationality,
  religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or
  serious illness.
- **Sexual content.** No pornography or sexually explicit material.
  **Any sexual content involving minors is banned immediately and reported
  to the authorities.**
- **Violence and gore.** No threats, no glorifying violence, terrorism or
  violent extremism, and no graphic images of death or injury.
- **Mocking victims.** No celebrating or making fun of people who died or
  were hurt in real events.
- **Self-harm.** No encouraging or promoting suicide, self-harm or eating
  disorders.
- **Private information.** Don't post anyone's home address, phone number,
  private photos or other personal details without their permission.
- **Illegal activity.** Nothing that breaks the law, including selling
  drugs or weapons.
- **Spam and scams.** No advertising, repeated posts, phishing links or
  get-rich-quick schemes.
- **Vote manipulation.** No extra accounts, vote trading or asking people
  to vote a certain way.
- **Impersonation.** Don't pretend to be another person or organisation.
- **Misinformation that causes harm.** No false claims about health
  emergencies, elections or tragedies that could lead people to get hurt.
- **Copyright infringement.** Only share things you have the right to
  share.

## Please do

- Stay on topic: the meme, its history, and the events around it.
- Credit the original creator when you know who made a meme.
- Tag posts with the right meme so they show up in the timeline.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Say when you're joking if it might not be obvious.
- Correct mistakes in the timeline. We want it to be accurate.

## What happens when guidelines are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

1. remove the post;
2. warn you;
3. suspend your account for a period;
4. ban your account permanently.

Serious breaches, such as sexual content involving minors or credible
threats of violence, lead to an immediate ban and may be reported to the
police.
